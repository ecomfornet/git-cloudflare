Fact-Checking Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Accuracy matters to us. Before publication, every article on Styleire goes through a review that checks each claim against a reliable source.

What we verify

  • Product specifications (dimensions, materials, safety ratings).
  • Design and construction claims (e.g. kiln-dried hardwood frames, rub counts).
  • Statistics and industry data, verified against primary or reputable secondary sources.
  • Direct quotes and attributions, verified with the source.

How to report an error

If you spot something that looks wrong, please email editorial@styleire.com with the article title and the specific claim. We investigate promptly, correct verified errors, and note the correction on the article.

Standard of proof

For safety-related claims, we require a primary source (manufacturer documentation, regulatory guidance, or peer-reviewed research). For style and design claims, we clearly signal opinion versus fact.
