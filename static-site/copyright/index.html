Text and editorial content

All written content is © 2026 Styleire. Articles are written and edited by our named editorial team, reviewed before publication, and updated over time. We hold first-publication rights to everything on this domain; none of it is syndicated from another publisher.

Photography and illustrations

What you may do with our content

  • Link to any page on this site freely, including in roundups and newsletters.
  • Quote up to roughly 75 words with a visible credit and a follow link to the original article.
  • Pin our images to Pinterest using the Pin/Save buttons on the page, which keep the link back to the source article.

What you may not do

  • Republish full articles or substantial portions of them, in any language, including AI-rewritten versions.

  • Use our brand name, logo, or editor identities to imply endorsement.

Requesting permission

Licensing and syndication requests are welcome. Email editorial@styleire.com with the URL, the specific content you want to use, and where it will appear. We usually reply within five business days.

DMCA / copyright complaints

If you believe content on this site infringes your copyright, email editorial@styleire.com with the subject "Copyright Notice" and include: (1) identification of the protected work, (2) the exact URL of the material you are reporting, (3) your contact details, (4) a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised, (5) a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act on their behalf, and (6) your physical or electronic signature. We investigate every complete notice and remove verified infringing material promptly.

Reporting infringement of our work

If you find our articles or photographs republished elsewhere without permission, please tell us at editorial@styleire.com so we can act on it.
