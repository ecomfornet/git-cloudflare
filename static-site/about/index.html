About Styleire

Styleire is an independent home decor publication. We publish practical guides, room-by-room ideas, and honest advice for people who want their homes to feel personal, functional, and lasting.

What we cover

We write about living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and dining rooms, outdoor spaces, DIY and craft projects, and seasonal decorating. Every guide is written with real homes in mind — apartments, starter houses, family homes, and everything in between.

How we work

Every article is written or reviewed by our senior editor, Sophia Bennett, a certified interior stylist with more than eight years of professional experience. Where we recommend techniques, we have tested them in real rooms. Where we cite research, we link to primary sources.

What we don't do

We don't run sponsored content dressed up as editorial. We don't recommend products we haven't seen or used. And we don't publish AI-generated filler — every article you read here is written by a person.

Contact

Have a question, correction, or story idea? Reach us at editorial@styleire.com or via our contact page.
